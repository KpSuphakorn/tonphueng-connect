## Decisions

- Status flow: `pending_payment` (รอยืนยันจ่ายเงินจำลอง) -> `paid` (รอร้านรับออเดอร์) after simulated confirmation -> Staff sets `preparing`, `shipping` or `delivered`. Staff may skip forward but not move backward.
- Tracking number: optional for delivery (letters, digits and ` . _ / -`, up to 60 characters; empty clears it); rejected for pickup in both the route message and PostgreSQL, and the Staff form hides the field for pickup.
- Payment is stored as `payment_method = 'simulated_demo'` and `payment_status` `pending_simulated`/`paid_simulated`, separate from fulfilment status.
- The create request carries only a request UUID and the store the Customer reviewed; items, prices, fee and owner come from the server cart. Retries after a lost response reuse the UUID.
